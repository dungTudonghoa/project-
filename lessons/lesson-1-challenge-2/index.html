Exercise 1: Complete these sentences by choosing the best preposition.
1. My house is ___ the sea. You walk out of the front door straight onto the beach. (by / in)
2. I live in a two-room flat ___ the outskirts of the city. (in / on)
3. We spent two weeks in a small village ___ the mountains. (in / at)
4. The town of Wadi Musa is ___ the desert. It's only an hour's drive away. (near / on)
5. When I was growing up in the UK, I lived ___ the suburbs. (in / on)
6. Our house was right ___ a river. There was even a place at the end of the garden where you could leave your boat. (by / near)
7. I would hate to live ___ the country. It must be so quiet and boring there. (by / in)
